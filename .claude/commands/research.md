# Market Research

Conduct market, competitor, and technology research with sourcing.

## Usage

```
/research "topic or company" [type: market|competitor|tech|investor]
```

## Output Structure

### Market Research
```markdown
# Market Analysis: [Topic]

## Market Size & Growth
- TAM/SAM/SOM estimates
- Growth rate (CAGR)
- Key drivers

## Market Segments
- Segment breakdown
- Target demographics
- Geographic distribution

## Trends
- Current trends
- Emerging opportunities
- Potential disruptions

## Sources
- [Source 1](url)
- [Source 2](url)
```

### Competitor Research
```markdown
# Competitive Analysis: [Company/Space]

## Landscape Overview
- Market leaders
- Challengers
- Emerging players

## Competitor Profiles
| Company | Product | Pricing | Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---------|---------|---------|-----------|------------|

## Differentiation Opportunities
- Gaps in market
- Underserved segments
- Feature opportunities

## Sources
```

### Technology Research
```markdown
# Technology Analysis: [Tech]

## Current State
- Maturity level
- Adoption rate
- Key players

## Technical Deep Dive
- Architecture/approach
- Pros and cons
- Implementation considerations

## Future Direction
- Roadmap/trajectory
- Emerging alternatives
- Recommended approach

## Sources
```

## Research Principles

1. **Always cite sources** - Include URLs for all claims
2. **Recency matters** - Prioritize recent data (< 2 years)
3. **Multiple sources** - Cross-reference key claims
4. **Quantify** - Numbers > vague statements
5. **Acknowledge gaps** - Note where data is limited

## Quality Checklist

- [ ] Key claims have sources
- [ ] Data is recent and relevant
- [ ] Competitive landscape is comprehensive
- [ ] Actionable insights included
- [ ] Limitations acknowledged
